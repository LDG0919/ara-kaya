Data Deletion

App: Ledger (돈잡이) · Package: com.korea919.ledger
Last updated: June 6, 2026

Ledger stores all data on your own device. You are in full control and can delete it at any time as follows.

1. Delete data on your device

  • Delete individual entries: select the entry in the app and delete it.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Ledger → Storage → Clear data. This permanently removes all locally stored entries.

2. Advertising data

If ads are shown, they are served by Google AdMob. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
