Privacy Policy

App: Ledger (돈잡이) · Package: com.korea919.ledger
Last updated: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ledger mobile app ("the App", "we") handles your information. Ledger is a personal money-tracking app. All of your ledger data is stored locally on your own device, and we do not operate any server that collects or stores your data.

1. Summary

  • Your entries (income/expense amounts, categories, dates, memos) are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect or receive your ledger data.
  • The App may show ads, which collect some data as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information the App handles

(a) Entries you create. Income/expense amounts, categories, dates, and memos. This is stored only in the App's local database on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

(b) Advertising. The App may use Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display ads. AdMob may collect and process data such as a device advertising identifier, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

(c) Permissions. The App may use Internet access to serve ads. Your ledger data is stored on the device regardless of Internet access.

3. How information is used

  • To store, display, and total your ledger entries on your device.
  • If ads are shown, to display and measure advertisements.

4. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your data. If ads are shown, data described in 2(b) may be shared with Google AdMob for that purpose only.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. For step-by-step instructions, see our Data Deletion guide.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Security

Your data is kept on your device. No method of storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
