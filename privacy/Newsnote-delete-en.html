Data Deletion

App: Newsnote (바람잡이) · Package: com.korea919.news
Last updated: June 6, 2026

Newsnote stores your data on your own device, and optionally in your own Google Drive if you enabled backup. You are in full control and can delete it at any time as follows.

1. Delete data on your device

  • Delete individual scraps: long-press a scrap to enter selection mode and delete it, or open a scrap and tap the trash icon.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Newsnote → Storage → Clear data. This permanently removes all locally stored scraps and images.

2. Delete your Google Drive backup

If you enabled Drive backup, delete the backup like this:

  1. Open Google Drive with the account you used.
  2. Find the "Newsnote" folder and delete it (then empty Trash to remove it permanently).

3. Revoke the app's access to your Google account

  1. In the app, open the side menu and tap Sign out; or
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Newsnote, and choose Remove access.

4. Advertising data

Ads are served by Google AdMob. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
