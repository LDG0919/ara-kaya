Square Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Square (“the Service”) is a co-op service where developers test one another's apps. We take your privacy seriously and collect, use, store, and delete personal information as described below.

A nickname is randomly assigned at sign-up, and your Google account's real name is not displayed in the Service. Among the contacts you enter, only the items you mark as “public” are visible to other users (for coordinating the co-op). “Private” items are visible only to you.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Member identification and authentication via Google sign-in
  • Co-op matching (creating/joining rooms) and attendance (app-launch) records
  • Providing a means of contact between users (only for contacts set to public)
  • Sending notifications (e.g., room closure) and operating/improving the Service
  • Ad delivery and measurement

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

  • To other users: contacts and nickname you set to “public” and the app information you register are shown to other users within the Service. Private items are not provided.
  • Advertising: the Service displays ads through Google AdMob, which may collect and use information such as your advertising ID for ad delivery and measurement. (Google Ads policy)
  • Except where required by law or upon a lawful request by authorities, we do not provide personal information to any third party other than the above.

4. Outsourcing and Overseas Transfer

For stable operation, the Service entrusts the processing of personal information to the following providers, whose servers may be located overseas.

ProviderEntrusted work
Google LLC (Firebase) Authentication, database (Firestore), file storage (Storage), server functions (Cloud Functions)
Google LLC (AdMob) Ad delivery and measurement

5. Retention and Deletion

  • Your personal information is deleted without delay upon account deletion.
  • Service data is cleaned up automatically: rooms 60 days after creation, notifications 60 days after receipt, and attendance records on roughly a 45-day basis.
  • Upon account deletion, account info, contacts, registered apps, room/ participation/attendance records, notifications, and your authentication account are removed from the server.
  • Where retention is required by law, data is stored separately for that period.

6. Your Rights

  • Access/Edit: you can view and edit your nickname, contacts, and public/private settings directly on the in-app profile screen.
  • Account deletion: you can delete your account and data yourself via “Delete account” at the bottom of the in-app profile screen.
  • For other inquiries, please contact us using the details below.

7. Advertising ID

You can control personalized ads in your device settings. Android: reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization under Settings > Privacy > Ads.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for developers and is not directed to children under 14. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 14, we will delete it without delay.
